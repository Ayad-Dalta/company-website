Our Foundation

Driven by Engineering Rigor & Uncompromised Safety

We combine advanced finite element modeling (FEA) with heavy industrial manufacturing to deliver bridges engineered to withstand extreme seismic, hydraulic, and load stresses.

Structural Longevity Guarantee

Every bridge design and structural steel batch is engineered for a minimum 100-year design life under severe environmental exposure, complying strictly with AASHTO LRFD and AISC codes.

Geotechnical Mastery

From deep karst foundation stabilization at the Mosul Dam to deep riverbed pier foundations in seismic fault zones, our geotechnical team resolves complex sub-surface challenges.

Digital Twin & Live Simulation

We deploy IoT strain gauges, fiber-optic tiltmeters, and real-time canvas physics solvers to continuously monitor bridge deflection and stress distribution in real time.

Our Journey

28 Years of Structural Breakthroughs

Key milestones in the growth of ApexBridge from a regional steel fabricator to an international heavy civil engineering authority.

1998

Foundation in Pittsburgh, PA

ApexBridge was established in Pittsburgh's industrial heartland, focusing on high-strength ASTM A572 Grade 50 structural steel fabrication for river crossing trusses across the Midwest.

2006

Prestressed Concrete & Bearing R&D Division

Expanded capabilities with an automated prestressed concrete yard and a dedicated elastomeric seismic bearing laboratory, earning AISC Major Steel Bridge Certification with Fracture Critical Endorsement.

2014

Digital FEA Engine & Sensor Monitoring

Pioneered real-time structural health monitoring (SHM) systems for cable-stayed bridges, integrating IoT strain sensors with proprietary Finite Element Analysis (FEA) algorithms.

2019 - 2022

Mosul Dam Geotechnical Stabilization

Mobilized heavy drilling equipment to northern Iraq to stabilize the Mosul Dam's karstic limestone foundation through continuous high-pressure grout injection and automated sensor grids.

2023 - 2026

Mosul Fixed Bridges Reconstruction & Procurement Portal

Reconstructed all 5 strategic Tigris River bridges in Mosul, Iraq, and launched our direct-to-contractor digital bridge materials e-commerce procurement portal.
