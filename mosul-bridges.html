CERTIFIED SUPPLY LOGISTICS

Structural Materials Deployed for Mosul Reconstruction

Key Engineering Interventions

  • Underwater Pier Foundation Underpinning: High-pressure grout injection into riverbed scour pits around damaged piers.
  • High-Strength Steel Truss Replacement: Fabricated 110m of ASTM A709 Grade 50W weathering steel trusses to restore Old Iron Bridge.
  • AASHTO HL-93 Heavy Axle Rating: Dynamic load testing with 45-ton test semi-trailers across all 5 spans.
  • UHPC Deck Waterproof Overlay: Applied 50mm Ultra-High Performance Concrete deck layer for 100-year anti-spalling protection.
